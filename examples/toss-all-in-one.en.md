## Toss · "All in one place" holds in the experience of using it

**Where the message travels**

From money to everyday life, in one place → many services moved inside the Toss app → they open with no install and no extra sign-up → **what the promise described is what happens**

| Connection | What is actually there |
|---|---|
| Message → app structure | Transfers, stocks, shopping, games, and weather all open inside one app |
| App structure → using it | Mini Apps need no download and no additional account |
| Using it → the screen | A new service uses the same interactions people already know from Toss |

**What the company says**

With its August 2026 homepage redesign, Toss put one line at the center: "금융부터 일상까지, 마침내 토스 하나로." The English site says the same thing. "From money to everyday life. All in one place." The claim is not that Toss built many services. It is that many things can be finished in one place. [Toss](https://toss.im/) · [Homepage redesign, 18 August 2026](https://www.newspim.com/news/view/20260818000868)

**What the product shows**

The Mini Apps page states three things plainly. No app download is required, no extra sign-ups or passwords are needed, and Mini Apps use familiar Toss patterns and interactions. Ordering food, checking an insurance premium, and playing a game all happen without creating another account. Shopping, weather, scheduling, and webtoons arrived the same way. [Toss Mini Apps](https://toss.im/service/miniapp)

Support is where the numbers divide. There are five: Toss Bank, securities, mobile plans, tax and refunds, and a line for anything else. The page itself opens with what went wrong rather than with product names - "I sent money to the wrong person", "lost or reissued card". Support runs 24 hours, with tax and refunds on weekdays only. [Toss support](https://support.toss.im/)

**Where it changes**

> **Verdict: the promise holds.**

- **Achieved:** no extra app to install, no extra account to open, many services used the same way inside one app
- **Verdict within the promise's scope:** holds
- **Separate consideration:** how quickly a call actually gets resolved
- **Caution:** do not use the number of support lines as evidence of failure

What Toss promised to bring together is the experience of using it, and that is what happened. You do not install a new app to shop, and you do not sign up again to play a game.

There are several support numbers. That looks less like something Toss failed to merge than something it split on purpose. A bank agent cannot answer a stock question. And for anyone who does not know which line to call, there is a line that takes anything.

**Why it may be this way**

**The blocking steps were removed outright**
Installing and signing up are where services usually split apart. Toss removed both in Mini Apps, and matched the interactions on top of that.

**Support looks divided by intent**
The lines are named after what you want to ask - securities, tax and refunds - not after which company runs them. Only tax and refunds, which needs a specialist, keeps different hours. A catch-all line exists for people who cannot classify their own question. That reads as a structure built to answer faster, not one that divides users.

> **Toss removed the split from the step where people use it, and split support on purpose so answers come faster.**

How quickly someone who calls actually gets resolved is not public. That does not overturn the verdict; it is the next thing to check.
